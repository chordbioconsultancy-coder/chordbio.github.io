What Services Do We Offer?

Below are examples of the types of projects that ChordBio Consulting can support. We work closely with clients to deliver tailored solutions that meet their specific needs and objectives. Please contact us to discuss your requirements and how we can help.

Quality Management Consulting

Development, implementation, and improvement of quality management systems, including review of SOPs, policies, and key documentation.

Laboratory Compliance and Sponsor Oversight

Good Clinical Practice for Laboratories (GCLP) compliance, sponsor oversight, and auditing of laboratories conducting clinical trial endpoint analyses.

Research Tissue Banking and Biobanking

Support for the establishment, governance, management, and regulatory compliance of tissue banks and biobanking operations.

Audit and Inspection Readiness

Good Clinical Practice audits, sponsor oversight,audits and inspection preparation, gap analyses, and corrective action planning.

Training and Coaching

Tailored training programmes and one-to-one coaching to strengthen quality, compliance, and operational excellence across teams.
